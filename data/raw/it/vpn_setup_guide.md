# VPN Setup Guide

Nordwerk Automotive GmbH · Version 3.0 · valid from 2026-01-15 · Owner: IT Infrastructure

## 1. When do I need the VPN?
You need the VPN to access internal systems (SAP, intranet, file shares) when you work outside a Nordwerk site. Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive) works without VPN.

## 2. Setup
2.1 The VPN client "GlobalConnect" is pre-installed on all company laptops.
2.2 Open GlobalConnect and enter the portal address `vpn.nordwerk.example`.
2.3 Sign in with your Nordwerk account and confirm the request in the Authenticator app on your phone.

## 3. Troubleshooting
3.1 "Portal not reachable": check your internet connection; hotel networks sometimes block VPN. Try a mobile hotspot.
3.2 "Authentication failed": your password may have expired. Reset it via PassReset.
3.3 Still not working: contact the IT Service Desk at extension 4400.

## 4. Rules
- Disconnect the VPN when you do not need internal systems.
- Never use the VPN on private devices.
