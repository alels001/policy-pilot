# IT FAQ for Employees

Nordwerk Automotive GmbH · Version 2026.2 · valid from 2026-05-01 · Owner: IT Service Desk

## 1. How do I reset my password?
Use the self-service portal "PassReset" from any company device. You need your registered mobile number for the verification code. If you are locked out, call the IT Service Desk at extension 4400.

## 2. How do I get access to a new software tool?
2.1 Open a request in the IT portal "ServiceNow" under "Software request".
2.2 Your manager approves the request.
2.3 Licensed software (e.g. CAD tools) additionally needs approval by the cost center owner.
2.4 Standard processing time is **3 working days** after approval.

## 3. Can I install software myself?
No. Employees do not have local administrator rights. Approved standard software can be installed from the "Software Center" without a ticket.

## 4. How do I get a new laptop or replacement device?
Laptops are replaced every **4 years**. Defective devices are replaced via a ticket in ServiceNow under "Hardware incident".

## 5. Can I use private USB sticks?
No. USB storage devices are blocked on company devices. Use OneDrive or Teams to share files.

## 6. Who do I contact?
IT Service Desk: extension 4400, Monday to Friday 7:00–18:00, it-help@nordwerk.example
