# Code of Conduct

Nordwerk Automotive GmbH · Version 2026 · valid from 2026-01-01 · Owner: Compliance

## 1. Our principles
We act with integrity, comply with laws and treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with respect.

## 2. Gifts and invitations
2.1 Employees may accept gifts from business partners only up to a value of **EUR 40** per gift and per year from the same partner.
2.2 Invitations to business meals are allowed if they are appropriate and have a clear business purpose.
2.3 Cash gifts and vouchers are never allowed, regardless of the amount.
2.4 Gifts above the limit must be reported to Compliance via compliance@nordwerk.example.

## 3. Conflicts of interest
Employees must disclose to their manager any private or financial relationship with a supplier, customer or competitor.

## 4. Confidential information
Internal and confidential information may only be shared with people who need it for their work. This includes salary data, audit results and security information.

## 5. Speaking up
Violations can be reported to the manager, to Compliance or anonymously via the whistleblower system "SpeakUp". Nordwerk does not tolerate retaliation against people who report in good faith.
