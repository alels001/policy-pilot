# Travel and Expense Policy

Nordwerk Automotive GmbH · Version 4.1 · valid from 2026-03-01 · Owner: HR / Finance

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all business trips of employees of Nordwerk Automotive GmbH, within Germany and abroad.

## 2. Approval
2.1 Every business trip must be approved in advance by the direct manager in the travel tool "TravelDesk".
2.2 Trips outside the EU additionally require approval by the department head.

## 3. Transport
3.1 Trains: 2nd class for trips up to 4 hours, 1st class for longer trips.
3.2 Flights: economy class. Domestic flights only if the train journey takes more than 5 hours.
3.3 Private car: EUR 0.30 per km, only if no cheaper option is reasonable.

## 4. Accommodation
Hotels are booked via TravelDesk. Upper limits per night including breakfast:
- Germany: EUR 130 (Munich, Frankfurt, Hamburg: EUR 160)
- Other EU countries: EUR 150
- Outside the EU: according to the country list in TravelDesk

## 5. Meal allowances (Germany)
5.1 Trips of more than 8 hours on one day: EUR 14.
5.2 Full day of a multi-day trip (24 hours): EUR 28.
5.3 Day of arrival and day of departure on multi-day trips: EUR 14 each.
5.4 If meals are provided (e.g. breakfast in the hotel), the allowance is reduced: breakfast by 20 %, lunch and dinner by 40 % each of the full-day rate.

Example: a two-day trip with one hotel night including breakfast results in EUR 14 (arrival day) + EUR 14 (departure day) − EUR 5.60 (breakfast) = EUR 22.40.

## 6. Expense claims
6.1 Expenses are claimed in TravelDesk within **4 weeks** after the trip.
6.2 Original receipts must be uploaded as a photo or PDF.
6.3 Reimbursement is paid with the next salary run.
